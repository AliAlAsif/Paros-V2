# Personal Academic & Research Operating System (PAROS)

## Role

You are an expert Full-Stack Software Engineer, UI/UX Designer, Database Architect, and Software Project Planner.

Your task is to build a professional, production-quality web application that serves as my personal academic operating system.

This is **not** a habit tracker. It is a long-term productivity platform designed to help me manage my university studies, research, exams, projects, and learning history.

The application must be modular, scalable, maintainable, and designed to grow over many years.

Do **not** generate the entire project at once. Build it step by step with my approval after each phase.
